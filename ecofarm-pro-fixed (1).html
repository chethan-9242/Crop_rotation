Resources & Tips

Essential knowledge for sustainable farming success

Daily Farming Tips

Soil Health Tip

Add compost regularly to improve soil structure and provide slow-release nutrients. Healthy soil is the foundation of sustainable farming.

Water Conservation

Use drip irrigation and mulching to reduce water usage by up to 50%. Water early morning or late evening to minimize evaporation.

Natural Pest Control

Encourage beneficial insects by planting diverse flowers. Ladybugs, lacewings, and parasitic wasps are natural pest controllers.

Crop Rotation Benefits

Rotate crops from different families to break pest cycles and improve soil nutrients. Never plant the same family in the same spot for consecutive seasons.

Companion Planting

Plant basil with tomatoes, marigolds with vegetables, and beans with corn. Companion plants can improve growth and repel pests naturally.
